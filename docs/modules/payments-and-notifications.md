# Payments and notifications contract

Responsibility: future payment monitoring, receipts, due-date events, notices,
and announcements. Inputs: approved obligations, provider results, administrator
content. Outputs: protected records and approved delivery attempts. Owned data:
future payment/receipt/notification records. Dependencies: provider, compliance,
consent, retention, and reconciliation decisions. Prohibited: payment handling,
provider selection, or notification channel setup before approval.
