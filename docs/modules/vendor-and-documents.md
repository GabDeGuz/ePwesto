# Vendor and documents contract

Responsibility: future vendor records and private submitted-document metadata.
Inputs: authorized submission and administrator review. Outputs: protected record
and explicit verification state. Owned data: vendor/document records. Interfaces:
reviewed storage and backend contracts. Dependencies: permissions, storage,
retention, and document policy. Acceptance boundary: access is authorized and
verification is manual. Prohibited: public storage, automatic verification, or
real documents in evidence.
